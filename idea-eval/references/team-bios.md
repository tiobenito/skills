## How this is used

When scoring **Team Fit**, the evaluator asks: does anyone here have a genuine edge for this
idea (a relevant skill, domain knowledge, or audience)? Or does it need capabilities the team
lacks (a big sales motion, regulatory expertise, physical ops)? Be honest — a high Team Fit
score should mean a real, nameable advantage, not enthusiasm.
