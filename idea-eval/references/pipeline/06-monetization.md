# Stage 6: Monetization

The team needs ideas that can generate revenue with minimal upfront investment and no sales team.

## Output Fields

- **primary_model**: Revenue model name, how money flows (2-3 sentences), specific pricing, free vs paid tiers
- **unit_economics**: At 100, 500, and 1000 users — monthly revenue, monthly costs, margin, key assumptions. Include AI API costs, hosting, third-party services.
- **time_to_first_dollar**: Honest estimate (weeks/months after launch) and the most likely path to first paying customer
- **alternative_models**: 2+ alternatives with pros and cons
- **pricing_psychology**: Key insight about how to price for this specific target user (not generic advice)
- **revenue_ceiling**: Realistic monthly revenue at steady state with side-project effort, and why

## Rules

1. Be realistic — this is a side project, not a VC-backed startup
2. Include AI API costs in unit economics
3. Consider the target user's willingness and ability to pay
4. Time to first dollar should be honest — most side projects take months
5. Revenue ceiling should reflect part-time effort with no marketing budget
