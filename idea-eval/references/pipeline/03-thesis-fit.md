# Stage 3: Thesis Fit (Scoring)

Score the idea on exactly these 8 criteria, each on a scale of 1-5.

## Team Context

- **Team:** A small group of technical generalists building AI-powered side projects. See `references/profile.md` for the actual team, constraints, and goals (built during onboarding).
- **Constraints:** Limited hours per week (typically 4-8 each), full-time jobs, bootstrap only.
- **Thesis:** Build AI-powered products that feel like magic to non-technical users. Two tracks: (1) quick consumer apps for short-term revenue, (2) niche SMB services for long-term recurring revenue.

## Scoring Guidelines

Be rigorous. Most ideas should NOT score 5 on everything. Use the full range:
- **1-2:** Weak on this dimension. Real concerns.
- **3:** Neutral. Nothing special, nothing terrible.
- **4:** Strong. Clear advantage on this dimension.
- **5:** Exceptional. Rare. Reserve for genuinely outstanding fit.

### AI Leverage
Does AI provide a 10x improvement, or is it bolted on? Could this be done without AI just as well?

### Competition
Consider both direct competitors AND substitute solutions (spreadsheets, manual processes, hiring someone). 1 = red ocean, 5 = blue ocean.

### Bootstrappability
Include not just money but time. Can an MVP ship quickly without significant capital?

### Revenue Clarity
Is there evidence people pay for this category? Or is it "nice to have"?

### Passive Potential
After launch, how much ongoing work? Customer support burden? Content creation needed?

### Team Fit
Reference `references/profile.md`. Is this something the team can realistically pull off? Does anyone on the team have a unique edge for this idea? Or does it require a large sales team, massive marketing budget, physical infrastructure, regulatory approvals, or other things that don't fit?

### Side Project Viability
Can it be maintained alongside full-time jobs without burning out?

### Market Timing
Is demand growing? Is the technology newly capable enough? Any regulatory tailwinds/headwinds?
