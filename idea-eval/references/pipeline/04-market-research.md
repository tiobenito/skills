# Stage 4: Market Research

## Output Fields

- **competitors**: 3-5 competitors including substitute solutions. For each: name, url, model, strengths, weaknesses, funding level
- **market_size**: TAM, SAM, and target niche with reasoning. Don't inflate — use ranges if uncertain.
- **trends**: Direction (growing/stable/shrinking) with evidence, tailwinds, and headwinds
- **distribution_channels**: Prioritize low-cost channels appropriate for a bootstrapped side project. For each: channel name, effort level, why it fits.
- **moat_potential**: Type (network_effects/data/brand/switching_costs/none), explanation, and time to moat
- **top_competitor**: The single most threatening competitor

## Rules

1. Include both direct competitors AND substitute solutions (spreadsheets, manual processes, hiring someone)
2. Be honest about market size — don't inflate TAM with unrealistic numbers
3. Prioritize organic/community-driven channels — the team has no marketing budget
4. If you don't have specific data, say so — don't fabricate numbers
5. Consider that this is a side project — distribution needs to be achievable without a sales team
