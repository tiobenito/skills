# Evaluator Profile

This file holds the context the evaluator scores ideas against — **your constraints, your
goals, and your team.** It drives the Team Fit and thesis-fit scoring.

It starts blank. **On first run, the skill interviews you to fill it in** (see "Setup —
build your profile" in SKILL.md), then saves your answers here so it never has to ask again.
You can also edit it directly any time your situation changes.

## How this is used

When scoring **Team Fit** (1.5x weight), the evaluator asks: does anyone here have a genuine
edge for this idea (a relevant skill, domain knowledge, or audience)? Or does it need
capabilities the team lacks? **Constraints** and **goals** shape Bootstrappability, Passive
Potential, Side Project Viability, and Revenue Clarity. A high score should mean a real,
nameable fit — not enthusiasm.
