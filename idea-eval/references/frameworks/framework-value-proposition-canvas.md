# Value Proposition Canvas

## Purpose
Align what you offer with what customers actually need by mapping customer jobs, pains, and gains to your value proposition.

## When to Use
- Validating new offers or products
- Refining messaging and positioning
- GTM strategy development
- When product-market fit feels off

## Process

### Part 1: Customer Profile

1. **Customer Jobs**
   - Functional jobs: Tasks they're trying to complete
   - Social jobs: How they want to be perceived
   - Emotional jobs: How they want to feel
   - Supporting jobs: Adjacent tasks in their workflow

2. **Customer Pains**
   - Obstacles preventing job completion
   - Risks and negative outcomes they fear
   - Frustrations with current solutions
   - What makes things harder or slower

3. **Customer Gains**
   - Required outcomes (must-haves)
   - Expected outcomes (should-haves)
   - Desired outcomes (nice-to-haves)
   - Unexpected delights (wow-factors)

### Part 2: Value Map

4. **Products & Services**
   - What you're offering (be specific)
   - Features and components

5. **Pain Relievers**
   - How you eliminate or reduce pains
   - Map each pain reliever to specific customer pains

6. **Gain Creators**
   - How you create or maximize gains
   - Map each gain creator to specific customer gains

### Part 3: Fit Analysis

7. **Assess the fit**
   - Which customer pains are you NOT addressing?
   - Which gains are you NOT creating?
   - Are you solving for what matters most?

8. **Prioritize**
   - Focus on extreme pains (severe + frequent)
   - Focus on essential gains (required outcomes)

9. **Synthesis**
   - Does your value proposition address top pains and create top gains?
   - What needs to change to improve fit?
   - How would you communicate this value clearly?
