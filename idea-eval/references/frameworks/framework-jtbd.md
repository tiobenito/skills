# Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)

**What it is:** People don't buy products, they "hire" them to make progress in a situation. Frame the idea around the job the user is trying to get done — functional, emotional, and social — rather than around features.

**Best for:** Sharpening who the user really is and what they'd switch away from.

## Running it

1. Describe the user's situation: what are they trying to accomplish, and in what context?
2. Write the job as: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]."
3. List what they "hire" today to do this job — including non-obvious substitutes (a spreadsheet, a person, doing nothing).
4. Ask what would make them fire the current solution and hire yours. What progress are they stuck on?

## What to pull out

- A crisp job statement and the real competing alternatives.
- The specific progress the user can't make today.
- Whether the idea is meaningfully better at the job, or just different.

## Watch for

- Describing features instead of the job.
- Defining the user as a demographic ("small businesses") instead of by their situation.
